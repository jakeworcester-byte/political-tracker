## Lessons learned
Weekly update, from the tracker's scorecard of called races ([scorecard](https://jakeworcester-byte.github.io/political-tracker/#results)):
- **Forecasters still missed in one direction.** The consensus rating was right in 141 races and wrong in 21, up from 139 and 20 last week. All 21 misses were races rated toward Republicans that Democrats won; the newest is NC-05 (Likely R). Democrats won 16 of the 17 races rated Tossup.
- **By forecaster:** Silver Bulletin was right on 89.0% of its non-Tossup calls while leaving only 7 races as Tossups. RealClearPolling scored highest, at 93.2%, but left 32 Tossups. The Economist was lowest, at 85.0%.
- **Polls:** with 15 tracked races now scored, polls missed by 3.2 points on average, and 3.0 points of that was toward underestimating Democrats. The biggest miss is still Texas Senate, where the average showed D+2.9 and the count shows D+12.1.
- **Markets and governors are unchanged:** Polymarket's final prices gave Democrats 94.5% for the House and 76.5% for the Senate, and Democrats won both. The governor consensus got 32 of 34 non-Tossup races right.
