# Midterm brief: Sunday, October 11

## Bottom line

Twenty-three days out, forecasters and markets agree on the shape of the race but not the outcome. The House leans Democratic: nine of ten forecasters on the Wikipedia ratings table project more Democratic than Republican seats before tossups, and Polymarket prices a Democratic House at 90.5% ([ratings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings), [market](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026)). The Senate is the contest: consensus ratings put Democrats at 46 seats solid or likely and Republicans at 45, with nine Lean-to-Tossup races deciding a 51-seat majority ([Senate ratings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Polymarket prices Democratic Senate control at 63.5% and a split Congress, Republican Senate with Democratic House, at 29.5% ([Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026), [balance of power](https://polymarket.com/event/balance-of-power-2026-midterms)).
