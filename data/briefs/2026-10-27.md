# Midterm brief: Tuesday, October 27

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, from Inside Elections' 212 D / 205 R to 230 D at The Economist, FiftyPlusOne and Silver Bulletin ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 10 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 91.5% for the House and 66.5% for the Senate, the Senate up two points ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 27.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 25, 1,583,181 ballots were cast, 136% of the 2022 total at the same point (1,163,784) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-26%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 26 the total reached 1,773,890 at D 41.0% / R 24.2%; the Democratic edge narrowed 1.2 points in a day ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Florida (Oct 26):** Registered Republicans now edge Democrats among 3,152,810 ballots, R 39.9% / D 39.5%, as in-person voting overtakes mail; registration is R 41.5% / D 30.1% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from Florida Division of Elections data).
- **Senate battlegrounds (Oct 26):** Arizona 956,224 at D 38.2% / R 38.7% (via a private compiler); Iowa 265,710 at D 50.6% / R 31.6%; Kansas 204,155 at D 46.9% / R 39.4%; Nebraska 151,915 at D 41.0% / R 42.2% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from secretary of state data).
