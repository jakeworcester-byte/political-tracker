# Midterm brief: Friday, October 30

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, and Inside Elections now has 224 D / 198 R with 13 tossups, up from 212 / 205 / 18 ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 47 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 9 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 93.5% for the House and 68.5% for the Senate, each up one point ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 30.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 28, 2,150,024 ballots were cast, 136% of the 2022 total at the same point (1,585,988) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-29%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 29 the total reached 2,353,234 at D 39.3% / R 25.7%; the Democratic edge narrowed 0.9 points in a day ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Florida (Oct 29):** 4,266,000 ballots at R 40.4% / D 38.6%, against registration of R 41.5% / D 30.1% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from Florida Division of Elections data).
- **Senate battlegrounds (Oct 29):** Arizona 1,295,955 at D 37.5% / R 38.6% (via a private compiler); Iowa 373,386 at D 47.8% / R 33.4%; Kansas 328,522 at D 43.3% / R 42.3%; Nebraska 200,455 at D 39.2% / R 43.8% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from secretary of state data).
