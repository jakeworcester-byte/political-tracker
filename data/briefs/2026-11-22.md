# Election update: Sunday, November 22 (morning)

## Bottom line
One more House race was called overnight. The House results table now marks 434 of 435 races called: D 261, R 173, with 218 needed for a majority ([House table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections)). The newest call is a flip: NC-05 went to Democrat Chuck Hubbard over Rep. Virginia Foxx in a seat forecasters rated Likely R. MN-01, the last race, is headed to a hand recount. Every Senate race is called: D 56, R 44, counting seats not up ([Senate table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Governorships are final at D 30, R 20 of 50, counting the 14 states with no race this year, with six flips, all to Democrats ([governor table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)). Eleven seats rated safe came in within 5 points; Democrats flipped TN-04 ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-elections/tennessee-us-house-district-4-results)), and Republicans held the other 10 (nine in the results table, plus CA-23 per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-elections/california-us-house-district-23-results)).
