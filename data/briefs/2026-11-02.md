# Midterm brief: Monday, November 2

## Bottom line
Markets price Democrats at 72.5% to win the Senate and 94.5% to win the House ([Polymarket Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026), [House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026)). The generic ballot average is D+8.6 across five aggregators as of Nov 1 ([Wikipedia aggregate table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections#Generic_congressional_ballot_aggregate_polls)). The weekend's story is Michigan: Democratic Rep. Haley Stevens withdrew her endorsement of Abdul El-Sayed and backed Republican Mike Rogers ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/01/nx-s1-6020993/michigan-senate-race-abdul-el-sayed-haley-stevens-mike-rogers)).

## What moved
- **Consensus changes (Senate):** Maine moved Lean D to Tossup; Minnesota and New Hampshire moved Likely D to Lean D ([ratings compilation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)).
- **Forecasters split on the final weekend.** In Michigan, Decision Desk HQ and FiftyPlusOne moved to Likely D while NBC News moved to Tossup. In Kansas, Split Ticket went to Lean D and NBC to Lean R ([same source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Caution: some of yesterday's 60-plus rating changes (Sabato's, RealClearPolling's) kept their old "as of" dates, so they may be table edits, not new releases.
- **NYT/Siena final polls (likely voters, ending Nov 1):**
  - [Michigan](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/11/01/polls/times-siena-mi-poll-toplines.html): El-Sayed 53, Rogers 42.
  - [Texas](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/11/01/polls/times-siena-tx-poll-toplines.html): Talarico 50, Paxton 43.
  - [Maine](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/11/01/polls/times-siena-me-poll-toplines.html): Jackson 51, Collins 45.
  - [Ohio](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/11/01/polls/times-siena-oh-poll-toplines.html): Brown 49, Husted 44.
  - [Kansas](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/11/01/polls/times-siena-ks-poll-toplines.html): Hamilton 49, Marshall 44.
  - [Iowa](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/11/01/polls/times-siena-ia-poll-toplines.html): Turek 48, Hinson 47.

  - The Michigan fieldwork ended the day Stevens switched, so it mostly predates her move.
- **Michigan average** moved from D+4.0 to D+5.3 ([tracker](https://jakeworcester-byte.github.io/political-tracker/#race/SEN-MI)).

## Races to watch today
- **Michigan (Lean D):** Silver Bulletin asks whether the Stevens switch could cost Democrats the Senate ([Silver Bulletin](https://www.natesilver.net/p/could-haley-stevens-cost-democrats)).
- **Maine (now Tossup consensus):** The University of New Hampshire has Jackson 54, Collins 45 (Oct 27–31) ([UNH](https://scholars.unh.edu/survey_center_polls/1013/)). The average is D+4.3.
- **Iowa (Tossup):** The average is R+0.1 across 9 polls, the closest of the tracked Senate races ([tracker](https://jakeworcester-byte.github.io/political-tracker/#race/SEN-IA)).
- **South Carolina (Lean R):** The WSJ reports polls show Graham tied with or trailing Annie Andrews ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/politics/elections/darline-graham-senate-race-da6227b0?mod=rss_socialpoliticsfeed)).

## Early vote
These are party registrations of voters who cast ballots, not votes.
- **North Carolina:** 2.82 million ballots, 131% of the 2022 total at the same point. Ballots are D 38.4 / R 26.2, against near-even registration (as of Oct 31, [NC State Board](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-11-01%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)).
- **Florida:** 5.40 million ballots, R+2.5, against R+11.4 registration (as of Nov 1, Florida Division of Elections via the [UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/)).
- **Pennsylvania** mail ballots are D+39.5, against D+1.5 registration (as of Nov 1, PA Department of State via the [UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/)). Mail voting skews Democratic.

## Talking points
- Democrats lead in all seven final NYT/Siena Senate polls; six of those seats are held by Republicans. Iowa and Alaska are within two points.
- Forecasters disagree more than usual heading into Tuesday; Kansas is rated anywhere from Lean D to Lean R.
- Expect a slow count: the NYT says some results could take days ([NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/02/us/politics/election-vote-counts.html)).
