# Midterm brief: Wednesday, October 28

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, and Sabato's Crystal Ball now has 225 D / 193 R with 17 tossups, Fox 223 D / 195 R ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 10 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 91.5% for the House and 66.5% for the Senate ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 28.

## What moved
- **Forecaster updates (Oct 27):** 47 rating changes, 45 toward Democrats ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). Fox News made 22 and Sabato 18, all toward Democrats.
- **House consensus:** FL-14 moved from Tossup to Lean D; PA-08 from Tossup to Tilt D; WI-01 from Lean R to Tossup; CO-05 from Lean R to Tilt R; AK-AL from Likely R to Lean R, so it joins the tracked list (62 races).
- **Senate:** Sabato and Fox moved South Carolina from Likely R to Lean R; Sabato moved North Carolina to Likely D; The Economist moved the Ohio special to Tossup while Fox moved it to Lean D ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). No Senate consensus changed; no poll average crossed a threshold.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 26, 1,773,889 ballots were cast, 137% of the 2022 total at the same point (1,298,679) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-27%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 27 the total reached 1,961,168 at D 40.4% / R 24.8%; the Democratic edge narrowed 1.2 points for a second day ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Florida (Oct 27):** 3,517,675 ballots at R 40.1% / D 39.1%, against registration of R 41.5% / D 30.1% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from Florida Division of Elections data).
- **Senate battlegrounds (Oct 27):** Arizona 971,184 at D 38.1% / R 38.8% (via a private compiler); Iowa 306,956 at D 49.4% / R 32.4%; Maine 220,814 at D 49.0% / R 21.7%; Nebraska 169,652 at D 40.3% / R 42.8% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from secretary of state data).
