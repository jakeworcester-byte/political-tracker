# Midterm brief: Thursday, October 15

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic: 9 of 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats than for Republicans, and Inside Elections is the exception at 209 D / 210 R ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 45 R seats are solid or likely, with 9 competitive. Minnesota joined the competitive list today and New Hampshire left it ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 90.5% for the House ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026)) and 63.5% for the Senate ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 15.
