# Midterm brief: Sunday, November 1

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, from Cook's 222 D / 195 R to 230 D at The Economist, FiftyPlusOne and Silver Bulletin ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 47 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 9 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 94.5% for the House and 72.5% for the Senate, the Senate up two points ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Nov 1.

## What moved
- **Maine Senate:** The consensus moved from Tossup to Lean D after Decision Desk HQ's change ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Average: D+3.3.
- **Forecaster updates (Oct 31-Nov 1):** 62 rating changes, 36 toward Democrats and 26 toward Republicans ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). Split Ticket made 38, including FL-11 and NC-13 again; those two seats have now flipped several times, so read Split Ticket's column cautiously. Decision Desk HQ made 12, 11 toward Democrats.
- **House consensus:** MI-10 moved from Lean D to Tossup; WI-01 from Lean R to Tossup; NY-01 from Lean R to Likely R, so it leaves the tracked list (61 races).
- **Senate ratings:** Split Ticket moved Michigan to Likely D; Decision Desk HQ moved Nebraska to Likely R. No poll average crossed a threshold.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 30, 2,612,249 ballots were cast, 132% of the 2022 total at the same point (1,975,607) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-31%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 31 the total reached 2,816,821 at D 38.4% / R 26.2% ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Florida (Oct 31):** 5,148,276 ballots at R 40.7% / D 37.7%, against registration of R 41.5% / D 30.1% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from Florida Division of Elections data).
- **Senate battlegrounds (Oct 31):** Arizona 1,563,442 at D 37.2% / R 38.1% (via a private compiler); Iowa 440,389 at D 46.2% / R 34.1%; Kansas 422,143 at R 43.6% / D 41.2% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from secretary of state data).
