# Midterm brief: Monday, October 26

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, from Inside Elections' 212 D / 205 R to 230 D at The Economist, FiftyPlusOne and Silver Bulletin ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 10 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 91.5% for the House and 64.5% for the Senate, the Senate down two points ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 26.

## What moved
- **Forecaster updates (Oct 25):** 36 rating changes, 19 toward Democrats and 17 toward Republicans ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). RealClearPolling made 16, 15 toward Democrats, moving MN-01, NV-02, OH-15, SC-01 and PA-10 to Tossup. FiftyPlusOne made 8, 7 toward Republicans.
- **Split Ticket reversal:** Split Ticket's seven House changes listed yesterday (CA-22, FL-07, FL-11, FL-22, NJ-07, NC-07, NC-13) all reverted to their prior ratings, so treat that pair of moves as noise. FL-11's consensus returned to Solid R.
- **Senate ratings:** RealClearPolling moved Nebraska and South Carolina from Lean R to Tossup; Silver Bulletin moved Iowa from Lean D to Tossup ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). No Senate consensus changed, and no poll average crossed a threshold.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 24, 1,544,673 ballots were cast, 137% of the 2022 total at the same point (1,130,865) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-25%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 25 the total reached 1,583,182 at D 41.6% / R 23.6% / other 34.8% ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Florida (Oct 25):** 2,783,995 ballots, now about half in person, at D 39.8% / R 39.5%, against registration of D 30.1% / R 41.5% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from Florida Division of Elections data).
- **Senate battlegrounds:** Arizona 884,474 at D 38.7% / R 38.4% (Oct 25, via a private compiler); Kansas 154,074 at D 47.7% / R 39.1% and Nebraska 136,433 at D 41.4% / R 42.0% (Oct 23, from the secretaries of state) ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/)).
