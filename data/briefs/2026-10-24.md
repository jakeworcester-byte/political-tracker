# Midterm brief: Saturday, October 24

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, and Cook now has 220 D / 198 R with 17 tossups, up from 214 / 200 / 21 ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 10 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 91.5% for the House and 67.5% for the Senate ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 24.

## What moved

- **Polls:** Nebraska Senate's average moved from R+1.0 to R+3.0 across 2 polls; TX-34 moved from D+3.0 to D+7.0 on one poll.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 22, 1,269,919 ballots were cast, 137% of the 2022 total at the same point (927,708) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-23%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 23 the total reached 1,453,517 at D 41.4% / R 24.0% / other 34.6% ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Large states (Oct 23):** Florida has 2,309,424 ballots (998,235 in person) at D 40.0% / R 39.5%, against registration of D 30.1% / R 41.5%; California 2,762,552 at D 50.1% / R 28.9%; Pennsylvania 771,806 at D 66.6% / R 24.3% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from secretary of state data).
- **Senate battlegrounds (Oct 23):** Arizona 759,605 at D 38.7% / R 38.5% (via a private compiler); Maine 178,680 at D 50.2% / R 21.3%; Iowa 207,774 at D 50.9% / R 32.0%; Nebraska 136,433 at D 41.4% / R 42.0% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/)).
