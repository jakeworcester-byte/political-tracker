# Midterm brief: Wednesday, October 14

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic: 9 of 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats than for Republicans, and Inside Elections is the exception at 209 D / 210 R ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)).
