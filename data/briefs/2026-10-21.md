# Midterm brief: Wednesday, October 21

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, and Sabato's Crystal Ball now has 223 D / 200 R with 12 tossups, up from 214 / 201 / 20 ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 10 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 91.5% for the House and 64.5% for the Senate ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 21.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 19, 733,299 ballots were cast, 138% of the 2022 total at the same point (532,240) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-20%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 20 the total reached 924,159, at D 43.8% / R 21.7% / other 34.5%; the Democratic edge narrowed 1.9 points in a day ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Large states (Oct 20):** Florida has 1,384,933 ballots (353,809 in person) at D 41.8% / R 37.5%, against registration of D 30.1% / R 41.5%; California 1,871,380 at D 49.2% / R 29.2%; Pennsylvania 635,088 at D 67.6% / R 23.7% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from secretary of state data).
