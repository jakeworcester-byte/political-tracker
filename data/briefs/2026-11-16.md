## Lessons learned
From the tracker's scorecard of called races ([scorecard](https://jakeworcester-byte.github.io/political-tracker/#results)):
- **Forecasters missed in one direction.** The consensus rating was right in 139 races and wrong in 20. All 20 misses were races rated toward Republicans that Democrats won, including the Mississippi Senate race (Likely R), the South Carolina Senate race (Lean R), and NC-06 (Solid R). Democrats won 15 of 16 races rated Tossup.
- **By forecaster:** Silver Bulletin was right on 89.3% of its non-Tossup calls while leaving only 7 races as Tossups. RealClearPolling scored highest, at 93.8%, but left 31 Tossups. The Economist was lowest, at 85.4%.
- **Polls underestimated Democrats.** In the 10 tracked races with a final polling average, polls missed by 4.2 points on average, all toward Republicans. The biggest miss was Texas Senate, where the average showed D+2.9 and the count shows D+12.1.
- **Markets:** Polymarket's final prices gave Democrats a 94.5% chance of the House and 76.5% of the Senate; Democrats won both.
- **Governors:** the consensus got 32 of 34 non-Tossup races right. The misses were Kansas (Tilt R) and Texas (Lean R), both won by Democrats.
