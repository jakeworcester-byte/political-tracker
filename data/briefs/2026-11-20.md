# Election update: Friday, November 20 (morning)

## Bottom line
No race was called overnight. The House results table marks 433 of 435 races called: D 260, R 173, with 218 needed for a majority ([House table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections)). Both remaining races are now in recounts: NC-05 and MN-01. Every Senate race is called: D 56, R 44, counting seats not up ([Senate table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Governorships are final at D 30, R 20 of 50, counting the 14 states with no race this year, with six flips, all to Democrats ([governor table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)).
