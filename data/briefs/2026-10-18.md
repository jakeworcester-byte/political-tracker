# Midterm brief: Sunday, October 18

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, from Inside Elections' 212 D / 205 R to FiftyPlusOne's and Silver Bulletin's 230 / 196 ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 10 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 90.5% for the House and 64.5% for the Senate ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 18.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 16, 437,217 ballots were cast, 134% of the 2022 total at the same point (326,248) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-17%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 17 the total reached 501,318, at D 45.4% / R 20.1% / other 34.6% against an even registration split ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Large states (Oct 16–17):** California has 1,313,465 mail ballots at D 43.7% / R 27.0%; Florida has 833,488 at D 42.5% / R 36.4%, against registration of D 30.1% / R 41.5%; Pennsylvania has 456,065 at D 68.7% / R 22.8% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from secretary of state data).
- **Senate battlegrounds:** Maine has 101,403 ballots at D 53.2% / R 19.4%; Nebraska 77,291 at D 44.8% / R 38.3%; Arizona 283,356 at D 40.1% / R 37.6%, via a private compiler ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/)).
