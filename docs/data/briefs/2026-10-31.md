# Midterm brief: Saturday, October 31

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, and Cook now has 222 D / 195 R with 18 tossups, up from 220 / 198 / 17 ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 47 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 9 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 94.5% for the House and 70.5% for the Senate, the Senate up two points ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 31.

## What moved

- **Polls:** Kansas Senate's average moved from D+1.5 to D+3.6 across 4 polls; MT-01's from D+2.2 to D+8.0 across 3.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 29, 2,353,233 ballots were cast, 134% of the 2022 total at the same point (1,753,484) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-30%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 30 the total reached 2,612,250 at D 38.7% / R 26.0% ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
