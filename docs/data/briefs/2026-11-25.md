# Election update: Wednesday, November 25 (morning)

## Bottom line
TN-04 is now marked called for Democrat Victoria Broderick in the results table, following AP, and The New York Times reports she won ([NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/24/us/politics/tennessee-house-election-results-broderick-wins.html)). That puts the House at 432 of 435 races called: D 259, R 173, with 218 needed for a majority ([House table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections)). Every Senate race is called: D 56, R 44, counting seats not up ([Senate table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Sen. Dan Sullivan (R) conceded the Alaska race to Mary Peltola (D), The New York Times reports ([NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/24/us/politics/dan-sullivan-concedes-alaska-senate-race-mary-peltola.html)). Governorships are final at D 30, R 20 of 50, counting the 14 states with no race this year, with six flips, all to Democrats ([governor table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)).

## Called since the last update
