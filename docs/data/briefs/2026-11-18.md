# Election update: Wednesday, November 18 (morning)

## Bottom line
Mary Peltola (D) won Alaska's Senate race, unseating Sen. Dan Sullivan (R), CBS News projects ([CBS News](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMic0FVX3lxTE5aMFg5T1RoMW1tRDV6MkRYRldkNTNjY1VnSlJ1bG1GaXE3cUkzcWMyRElPOURHSXBUQWFIVjlfRGM0OG9Cci02ajZqbElsOE9UV2hjVDRmekRldURKeXp3WHRzT1luVFJ4SEdZLU1Fc1ctNkk?oc=5)). She cleared a first-choice majority in the state's count, so the race was decided without ranked-choice rounds ([Alaska Division of Elections](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/enr/26GENR/results/ElectionSummaryReportRPT.pdf)). Every Senate race is now called: Democrats hold 56 seats to Republicans' 44, counting seats not up ([Senate table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). The House table marks 432 of 435 races called: D 260, R 172, with 218 needed for a majority ([House table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections)). Governorships are final at D 30, R 20 of 50, counting the 14 states with no race this year, with six flips, all to Democrats ([governor table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)). Eight seats rated safe came in within 5 points; Democrats flipped TN-04, and Republicans held the other seven (six in the results table, plus CA-23 per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-elections/california-us-house-district-23-results); TN-04 per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-elections/tennessee-us-house-district-4-results)).
