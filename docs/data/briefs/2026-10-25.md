# Midterm brief: Sunday, October 25

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, from Inside Elections' 212 D / 205 R to The Economist's and Silver Bulletin's 230 D ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 10 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 91.5% for the House and 66.5% for the Senate, the Senate down one point ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 25.

## What moved

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 23, 1,453,516 ballots were cast, 136% of the 2022 total at the same point (1,067,593) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-24%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 24 the total reached 1,544,674 at D 41.5% / R 23.8% / other 34.7% ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Large states (Oct 24):** Florida has 2,597,716 ballots (1,220,521 in person) at D 39.8% / R 39.7%, against registration of D 30.1% / R 41.5%; California 2,943,863 at D 47.0% / R 27.1% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), from secretary of state data).
- **Senate battlegrounds:** Arizona 861,138 at D 38.4% / R 38.7% (Oct 24, via a private compiler); Iowa 207,774 at D 50.9% / R 32.0% and Nebraska 136,433 at D 41.4% / R 42.0% (Oct 23) ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/)).
