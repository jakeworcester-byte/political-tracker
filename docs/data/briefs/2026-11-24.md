## Bottom line
**The tracker now counts House races on AP's calls.** That puts the House at 431 of 435 races called: D 258, R 173, matching AP's count of 258 to 173 with four races undecided ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/democrats-clinch-historic-house-majority-chuck-hubbard-defeats-longtime-gop-rep-virginia-foxx)).
