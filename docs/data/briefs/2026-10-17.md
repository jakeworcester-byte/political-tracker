## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 15, 237,541 ballots were cast, 125% of the 2022 total at the same point (189,375) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-16%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 16 the total reached 437,214, at D 45.4% / R 20.6% / other 34.0% against an even registration split; the Republican share is rising as in-person voting grows ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/)).
- **Florida and Pennsylvania (Oct 16):** Florida has 816,890 mail ballots at D 42.5% / R 36.3%, against registration of D 30.1% / R 41.5%. Pennsylvania has 456,065 at D 68.7% / R 22.8% ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/), state data).
- **Senate battlegrounds (Oct 16):** Maine has 101,403 ballots at D 53.2% / R 19.4%; Iowa 33,417 at D 53.9% / R 30.0%; Arizona 165,881 at D 40.7% / R 37.1% (via a private compiler) ([UF Election Lab](https://election.lab.ufl.edu/early-vote/2026-early-voting/)).
