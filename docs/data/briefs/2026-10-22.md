# Midterm brief: Thursday, October 22

## Bottom line
Forecasters rate the House as leaning Democratic; all 10 House forecasters have more seats rated for Democrats, from Inside Elections' 212 D / 205 R to The Economist's 230 / 190 ([Wikipedia ratings table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)). The Senate is closer: 46 D and 44 R seats are solid or likely, with 10 competitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)). Markets price Democratic control at 92.5% for the House and 66.5% for the Senate, both up from yesterday ([Polymarket House](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-house-in-2026), [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/which-party-will-win-the-senate-in-2026)), as of Oct 22.

## Early vote
These are ballots by party registration, not votes, and mail ballots have leaned Democratic since 2020.
- **North Carolina:** Through Oct 20, 924,160 ballots were cast, 138% of the 2022 total at the same point (668,913) ([NCSBE](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/Press/2026%20General/NC%20Absentee%20Stats%20for%202026%20General/2026-10-21%20Daily%20Absentee%20Stats%20Report%20-%202026%20General.pdf)). By Oct 21 the total passed 1.1 million at D 43.0% / R 22.5% / other 34.4%; the NYT reports new rules threaten thousands of mail ballots ([NCSBE data file](https://dl.ncsbe.gov/?prefix=ENRS/2026_11_03/); [NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/21/us/politics/north-carolina-senate-mail-ballot-rejections.html)).
